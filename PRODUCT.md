# Berlin Cafe & Bistro

<!-- Written from the client brief without a separate interview (assumptions labelled). -->

## Product
Berlin Café & Bistro is a cafe and all-day bistro in Mirpur, Dhaka, established 2026. Specialty coffee ("The Coffee Chapter"), organic blended teas, a German-named bakery counter ("Die Bäckerei") and a bistro kitchen (appetizers, sandwiches and wraps, salads, breakfast). House line: "Brewed for the restless."

## Audience (assumption)
Dhaka cafe-goers aged roughly 18-40: students, young professionals and friends looking for a place to sit, work or spend an evening. They mostly arrive from Instagram and Facebook on a phone.

## Jobs of the website
1. Make the cafe feel premium and worth visiting.
2. Let people browse the full menu with prices and photos.
3. Get them to the cafe: hours, location, socials.

## Brand commitments
- Logo badge: charcoal with bronze rope border, white serif "BERLIN", classical temple, laurel, coffee cup. Est. 2026.
- Interior: exposed brick, black walls, warm pendant lamps, checkerboard floor, marble tables.
- Owner loves the vintage parchment menu with ornate framed dish photos and "350/-" prices.
- Client asked for: scroll-driven bean-to-cup hero ending in the real Berlin cup, separate menu page, extremely animated, classy, Apple-like glass.

## Open facts to confirm
- Opening hours: opening poster says 9 AM to 11 PM; a menu draft says 8 AM to 10 PM. Site uses 9 AM to 11 PM.
- Street address (only "Mirpur, Dhaka" plus a Maps link known).
